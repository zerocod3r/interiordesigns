S-39 Panchsheel

Painted panels and arched marble

A stair hall in arched marble sets the key for the whole house. Panels are hand-painted rather than papered, blossom and bird drawn at the scale of the room, and the light fittings are read as branches rather than fixtures.

Bedrooms take the same language down a register — softer colour, quieter joinery, a bench at the foot of every bed. The bathrooms answer in stone: a single vein carried across floor, wall and vanity, with brass drawn thin enough to disappear.
