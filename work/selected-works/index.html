Selected Works

Entrances, lobbies and a family mandir

Three rooms drawn for other houses, kept together because each one is about the moment of entering — a double-height lobby with a gallery running around it, a foyer where a sculpted brass panel does the work of art, and a mandir held in white marble.

The mandir is the quietest of the three. Everything is stone and light: an inlaid lotus in the floor, a leaf-brushed ceiling, and one chair, because someone will want to sit.
