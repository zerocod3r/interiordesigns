Trilok Residence

A sculptural arrival

The house begins before the front door. Cars pull into a stone-lined porch under a single folded canopy, past a bronze form standing in water. The floor runs straight through to the lift lobby without a change of material, so arrival reads as one movement.

Inside, the geometry softens. Lounges are drawn as curves — a bouclé sofa bent around a low table, a ribbon of light overhead. The private floors are quieter: a bedroom in bronze relief, a wardrobe gallery in fluted glass, a bathroom carved from a single run of marble.
