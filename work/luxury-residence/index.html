Luxury Residence

Walnut, marble and candlelight

The brief was an apartment that behaves like a house: rooms that close for an evening and open again for a morning. Everything is built around the fireplace wall — book-matched marble, a chandelier hung low enough to read by, and seating drawn in a circle rather than a row.

Away from the formal rooms the palette drops into walnut and oxblood. A red-marble bar, a chess table under a coffered ceiling, a dining room in pale stone. At the centre of the plan a courtyard holds a single blossoming tree, so that every corridor ends in daylight.
