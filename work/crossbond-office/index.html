Crossbond Office

A workplace that behaves like a home

An office floor detailed with the materials of a house. Oak laid in herringbone, cabinetry in a single warm tone, and a cove of light run around the ceiling so nothing overhead glares.

Cabins are glazed but curtained in soft neutrals; the open floor keeps a lounge at its centre rather than a corridor. Storage does the work of walls, and every desk sits within reach of daylight.
